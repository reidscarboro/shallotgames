What is Coffee Golf?

The perfect morning companion for golf enthusiasts

Coffee Golf is designed to be a meditative daily ritual that pairs perfectly with your morning routine. Unlike traditional golf games that focus on complex mechanics and progression systems, Coffee Golf strips the experience down to its purest elements: strategy, precision, and the satisfaction of a well-planned round.

Each day brings a completely new 5-hole course that's the same for all players worldwide. This creates a unique shared experience where you and your friends can compare strategies and scores on identical challenges.

The game's innovative "play holes in any order" mechanic transforms each course into a strategic puzzle. Do you tackle the difficult island green first while you're fresh, or save it for last? The choice of route through the course can make the difference between a par round and something truly special.
